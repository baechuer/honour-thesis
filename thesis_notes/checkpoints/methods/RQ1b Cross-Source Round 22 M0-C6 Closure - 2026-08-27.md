# RQ1b Cross-Source Round 22 M0-C6 Closure

Date: 2026-08-27

## Boundary

This checkpoint records public-source discovery, local source staging,
source-only structural curation, prompt cue control, model-assisted blinded
adequacy review, and strict-packet freezing. It is not human annotation,
retrieval or representation evaluation, embedding or reranking evidence, an
API result, a metric, or downstream-task success.

## M0 Through C1

- M0 recorded 127 public GitHub leads, canonicalised them to 123 roots, and
  excluded 23 roots already admitted in earlier discovery rounds, leaving 100
  exact-new candidates for M1.
- M1 HEAD-pinned and tree-censused the candidate roots. The bounded source
  cohort staged 585 byte-verified, frontmatter-valid source documents from 38
  origins without executing source code; exact duplicate source bytes were
  preserved in provenance but removed from source navigation.
- M2 derived source-only navigation metadata. Four independent C0A domain
  lanes proposed seven three-candidate navigation compositions. C0B reviewers
  reviewed their exact original-source packets and exact-validated 54 literal
  evidence substrings: six proposals were structural rejects and one
  document-authoring composition was retained only as a source-backed draft.
- C1 rechecked that draft's three source hashes, three distinct origins,
  candidate count, and no-reuse relation. These are source/integrity facts,
  not prompts, labels, or results.

## C2 Through C6

- C2 drafted six direct/paraphrase packets, two per candidate. C3's final
  literal cue audit passed all six; the manual C3 ledger records medium or
  high residual operational-context risk rather than presenting any packet as
  cue-free or as evidence of implicit-intent routing.
- C4 presented six anonymous candidate-card packets to two independent,
  source-deidentified, model-assisted reviewers (not human). Four packets had
  exact singleton agreement. Two packets remained reviewer disagreements
  because one reviewer considered an additional candidate fully adequate;
  they were not repaired or forced into strict top-1.
- C5 unsealed the four exact-singleton packets and confirmed each against its
  sealed C2 construction target. C6 rechecked the C1 composition/hashes, C3
  manual coverage, and C4/C5 chain, freezing four strict packets from the one
  three-candidate composition.
