## Source-Only Triage

Independent source-only reviewers read selected raw artifacts without prompts,
labels, selector outputs, metrics, or prior C1 outcomes. The following
families were rejected before D1 because the artifacts were lifecycle stages,
containers, components, or multiple-adequate alternatives: data migration,
API/testing, contract review, literature review, scientific writing,
incident response, accessibility/UI audit, and interface design.

Three conservative D1 candidate compositions were materialised with exact
source evidence: security review, compliance assessment, and presentation
authoring. The first materialisation attempt retained a local failure record
because one evidence span was truncated; a second attempt used the full
literal source sentence and passed hash/span validation. Neither attempt
created a scientific input or a routing result.

C1. No composition proceeds to C2.
