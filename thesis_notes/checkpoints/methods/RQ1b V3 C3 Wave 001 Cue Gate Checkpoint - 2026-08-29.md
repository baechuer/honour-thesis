## What This Does And Does Not Establish

C3 records cue control and residual cue risk only. It establishes neither a
strict gold label nor that a prompt has one fully adequate candidate. It does
not establish card fidelity, selector performance, a field effect, semantic
routing, ecological validity, or any RQ1 result.

The next gate is C4. Before key-blind singleton review, each source must be
represented as a source-deidentified, seven-slot card built solely from exact
contiguous source evidence and independently literal-audited. C4 reviewers
will see the prompt and anonymous cards, never source IDs, titles, the sealed
construction target, selector outputs or metrics. `MULTIPLE_ADEQUATE`,
`NONE_ADEQUATE`, disagreement or unsafe card construction remains excluded
from strict Top-1.
