# RQ1 Exhaustive Ablation Unified Primary Amendment

Date: 2026-08-31

Status: `PROSPECTIVE METHOD AMENDMENT / NO SCORING`

The public full-original experiment remains one experiment. Every target-clear
unit enters the same primary `FULL` versus `TARGET_ABLATION` comparison. It
estimates routing reliance on an information family as the field is naturally
encoded in a public skill document, including mixed or distributed carriers.

Natural field density is not an exclusion criterion. Every row reports removed
proxy tokens, removed fraction, near-empty status and matched-control
availability. A large deletion is interpreted as evidence that the information
family is deeply integrated with the document, not as a perfectly isolated
slot intervention.

The 147 deterministic damage controls are retained as supporting sensitivity
evidence where mechanically valid. Six of 14 pilot units have matched controls
for every candidate. Control availability does not create a second research
question, stratum or primary population, and unmatched output/workflow units
remain in the unified primary analysis.

The claim boundary is explicit: the primary effect shows what happens to
routing when all recoverable carriers of a field are removed. It does not by
itself separate the field's semantics from every collateral effect of deleting
mixed text. The controlled RQ1 experiment remains the cleaner isolated-field
sufficiency test.
