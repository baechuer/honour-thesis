`MAP_READY` means all candidates are `SAFE_MAP` or `NO_DIRECT_VALUE`, and at
least one explicit value will be edited. It is only permission to construct a
deterministic source-faithful removal. It does not say that the final masked
document is free of residual values, that it belongs to a strict routing
family, or that the removed field changes routing.

`ORIGINAL_ONLY` is conservative: one or more candidate values cannot be
removed from their original textual carrier without removing non-target
content or introducing an unsupported transformation. It is a natural-document
structure result, not evidence against the routing value of that field.
