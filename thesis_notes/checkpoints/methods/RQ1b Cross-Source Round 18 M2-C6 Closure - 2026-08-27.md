# RQ1b Cross-Source Round 18 M2-C6 Closure

Date: 2026-08-27

## Boundary

This checkpoint closes the Round 18 small-source cohort after its prior M0-M1
intake checkpoint. It records source-navigation sorting, source-only structural
screening, prompt cue control, blinded model-assisted adequacy review, stratum
assignment, and packet freezing. It does not record human annotation,
retrieval, representation scoring, embedding, reranking, an external API call,
a metric, or downstream-task success.

## M2 Through C1

- M2 generated names, descriptions, and heading previews for all 1,120
  canonical originals. It made no similarity, candidate, prompt, or label
  decision.
- Five disjoint C0A navigation curators proposed nine 3-candidate source-only
  compositions. The local merge checked IDs, candidate count, context coverage,
  and a two-origin minimum only.
- C0B reviewers inspected the 27 exact original artefacts. The validated
  ledger contains 46 literal source-evidence substrings: seven proposals were
  structural rejects and two were retained as source-backed drafts.
- The rejects are not low retrieval scores. They document true source-level
  containment, generic-plus-specialist asymmetry, lifecycle mismatch, or an
  absent bounded peer envelope.
- C1 passed both retained 3-candidate drafts: hashes are intact, each has at
  least two origins, candidate source hashes are distinct, and none reuses a
  candidate in any earlier frozen C6 manifest.

## C2 Through C6

- The two C1-passing drafts produced 12 direct/paraphrase packets.
- C3 initially found two literal three-token phrase overlaps. Both prompt
  fragments were reworded; final literal audit passed 12/12. The manual C3
  ledger records all final packets as `high` residual operational-context risk:
  their target constraints remain explicit even though title and copied-phrase
  cues were removed.
- C4 materialised anonymous A-C cards without source identities and kept the
  card-to-skill mapping in a separate sealed key. Two independent
  model-assisted, non-human reviewers returned 12/12 exact singleton
  agreements and no multi-adequate or disagreement packet.
- The returned C4 adequacy decisions are retained in a compact decision map;
  a local transcription materialised exact evidence snippets from the same
  blind cards without reading the sealed key or changing any adequacy value.
  This citation-normalisation detail is preserved for audit rather than being
  represented as an additional independent review.
- C5 unsealed the mappings and found that all 12 singleton cards match their
  C2 construction targets. C6 revalidated the C1/C3/C4/C5 chain and froze two
  candidate compositions and 12 primary strict packets.

## Running Strict Curation State

The historical C6 manifests now total 29 cross-source candidate compositions
and 188 strict direct/paraphrase packets. These are curation and feasibility
counts, not a retrieval accuracy result or proof that user intent is implicit.

The two newly frozen source-backed compositions are:

- product, B2B-account, and SaaS-business health diagnosis; and
- executive-Python, manuscript-refinement, and R/biology publication-figure
  production.
