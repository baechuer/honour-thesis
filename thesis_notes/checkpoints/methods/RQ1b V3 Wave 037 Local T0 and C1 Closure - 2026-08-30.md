# RQ1b V3 Wave 037 Local T0 And C1 Closure

Date: 2026-08-30

## Scope

Wave 036 searched first for new public roots, but its predeclared M1 requests
stopped at GitHub HTTP 403 rate limiting. Wave 037 is therefore a zero-network
contingency over the already frozen source frame. It is source-only curation
feasibility, never an RQ1b retrieval, information-field, prompt, label,
selector, metric, or thesis result.

## T0 Preparation And Result

- 10,000 retained distinct-title lexical drafts were scanned locally.
- 1,971 drafts touching an historical C0 or V3 C6 source reference were
  excluded; 8,029 remained eligible.
- Greedy selection formed 18 source-disjoint triads over 54 originals, each
  rehashed to the frozen canonical frame. Lexical and title signals ordered
  reading only.
- Six independent reviewers screened three triads each, seeing original source
  artefacts and the T0 protocol only.
- Literal-valid T0 ledger: 13 `LIKELY_NONPARALLEL`, two
  `NO_PLAUSIBLE_TRIAD`, and three `READY_FOR_C1`.

## C1 Closure

Each of the three C1 packets had two independent reviewers. All nine packet
sources rehashed to their frozen roster entries, and all six return files
passed exact-span literal audit. The final dispositions are:

| Packet | Source family | Final C1 outcome | Why it stops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `C1-001` | pgvector | `REJECT_NONPARALLEL_OR_COMPONENT` | General pgvector implementation, similarity/indexing guidance, and an Azure/pg_diskann variant are not independent direct first routes. |
| `C1-002` | caching | `REJECT_NO_C1_ADVANCE_CONSENSUS` | Both reviewers reject advancement: broad cache strategy/invalidation routes overlap; their reject categories differ, so no C2 advance can be inferred. |
| `C1-003` | Google Ads PMax | `REJECT_NONPARALLEL_OR_COMPONENT` | Maturity evaluation, broad optimisation, and downstream analysis differ in role; one analysis feeds other drafts. |

No C2 prompt may be constructed from this wave. The active strict V3 cohort
stays **37 frozen compositions / 231 strict prompt cases**.
