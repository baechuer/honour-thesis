curation, prompt cue control, model-assisted blinded adequacy review, and

## M0 Through C1

- M0 recorded 173 public GitHub navigation leads, canonicalised them to 150
  roots, and excluded roots already represented in earlier intake before M1.
  These records are discovery leads only.
- M1 commit-pinned reachable source roots, tree-censused their candidate
  `SKILL.md` paths, and locally staged exact source bytes without executing
  source code. M2 retained 1,419 canonical navigation records from 60 origins;
  declared-licence status was captured as provenance and was not used as an
  eligibility exclusion.

  reviewers found one source-backed draft suitable for further gates: a
  three-origin software data-protection-compliance composition. The other 20
  reviewer structural-reject dispositions remain pending batch-transcript
  integration and are not counted here as fully integrated ledger records.
- C0B exact-evidence validation and C1 then passed the advanced draft's three
  source hashes, three origins, candidate size, and no-reuse check. This is a
  source/integrity fact, not a prompt, label, or result.

## C2 Through C6

- C2 drafted six direct/paraphrase user-intent packets, two per candidate. C3
  literal-cue audit passed 6/6 with zero candidate-name, title/short-line, or
  exact three/four-token overlap findings. Manual C3 records retain `medium`
  residual operational-context risk for the EU-governance and Nigerian cases,
  and `high` risk for the platform-specific application cases. These are not
  evidence of implicit-intent routing.
- C4 materialised six anonymous reviewer packets and a separately sealed key.
  Two independent source-deidentified, model-assisted reviewers (not human)
  reviewed every anonymous card in every packet. The first local consensus

  Markdown heading level; the excerpts were repaired against the same blind
  cards without changing any adequacy judgment. The final exact-evidence audit
  recorded six exact singleton agreements, zero multi-adequate agreements,
  and zero disagreements.
- C5 unsealed after the blind-consensus audit and verified all six singleton
  cards against their sealed C2 construction targets. C6 rechecked C1 source
  composition/hashes, C3 manual coverage, and C4/C5 chain, then froze six
  strict packets from one three-candidate composition.
