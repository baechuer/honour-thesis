- C2 produced 36 direct/paraphrase packets. The final literal C3 audit passed

- Parent C3 adjudication applied the frozen rule rather than relabelling that

  context; the source-informed warnings remain attached. Parent residual risk

- C4 produced 22 exact singleton agreements, seven multiple-adequate
  agreements, and seven reviewer disagreements. C5 verified that every one of

- C6 rechecked C1 hashes and candidate composition, final C3 coverage,
