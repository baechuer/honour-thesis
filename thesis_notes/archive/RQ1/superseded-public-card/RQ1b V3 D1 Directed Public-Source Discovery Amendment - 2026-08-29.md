## Why A Separate Amendment

The V3 C0 lexical queue is retained as a feasibility audit. Its title and
local-FTS similarities are useful for finding broad topical neighbours, but
they frequently surface a generic container together with a lifecycle component
or an interface-only variant. That outcome is informative and must remain
reported as a separate discovery path; it must not be hidden by a later,
better-targeted queue.

D1 therefore changes only *candidate discovery*. It does not relax the
source-evidence, cue-control, blinded singleton, or freeze gates of V3-C1 to
V3-C6. No D1 candidate is a cluster, a gold label, a prompt, an information
field annotation, a selector input, or a result.

## Discovery Unit

A D1 draft is a 3--4 member composition of canonical, byte-distinct public
original skill artifacts. The members may come from mixed public origins; each
origin and SHA-256 binding remains visible. A same-origin composition is not
automatically rejected, but it is marked so later reporting can distinguish it
from cross-origin compositions.

The discovery priority is an explicit, source-visible routing constraint that
creates plausible alternatives under one operational envelope, for example:

- the same legacy asset with distinct target systems or dialects;
- the same artifact type with a distinct required output representation;
- the same broad deployment or integration goal with a distinct required
  provider, runtime, interface, or compatibility boundary; or
- the same diagnostic goal with a source-visible prerequisite that makes one
  route applicable and another inapplicable.

These are discovery hypotheses only. They do not claim that the corresponding
I3 field is causal or independently sufficient in natural documents.

## Local D1 Pre-Triage

For every prospective member, the local packet must bind its immutable source
and record literal source spans for:

1. a request trigger or material input;
2. its core operation;
3. its output or resulting state; and
4. a target, boundary, prerequisite, dependency, or route-out when stated.

The packet is rejected before C1 if a member is visibly a generic container,
wrapper, lifecycle step, duplicate, style-only overlay, or interface-only
implementation of another member. A shared product word alone is insufficient
for a common envelope.

## Gate Sequence

1. D1 materialises source-only candidate packets from the frozen 2026-08-29
   source frame. It may use local headings, source text and source-origin
   metadata, but no prompt, label, selector output, web access, or external
   model/API.
2. A source-only reviewer checks the packet against the D1 pre-triage rules.
   A survivor is passed to the unchanged V3-C1 exact-span source-evidence
   gate, not to prompt construction.
3. Only a C1 pass can enter C2 prompt construction, followed by the unchanged
   C3 cue control, two independent C4 singleton reviews and C5/C6 freezing.

## Reporting Boundary

Report lexical-C0 and directed-D1 screening counts separately. Neither rate is
a prevalence estimate for public skills, a strict-cluster yield, or a routing
performance result. The only eligible strict-public benchmark count is the
number surviving C1--C6.
