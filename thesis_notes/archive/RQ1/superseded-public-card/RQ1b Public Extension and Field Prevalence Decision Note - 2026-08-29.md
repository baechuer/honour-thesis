## Deferred Line A: RQ1b v3 Public Near-Neighbour Expansion

The prospective goal is a separate cohort with at least 100 frozen public
candidate compositions in total, not 100 routing families misreported as
independent libraries. New source-grounded 3--4-candidate compositions would
be discovered and reviewed under the same strict-singleton, two-prompt,
source-hash, cue-risk, no-reuse and exclusion rules as v2. Because the strict
gates intentionally reject nonparallel, no-adequate and multiple-adequate
cases, discovery must over-recruit drafts; no final yield is assumed in
advance.

V3 would be reported separately first. Pooling with v2 would require a
predeclared compatibility check after both cohorts are frozen.

## Deferred Line B: Source-Stratified Public Field-Prevalence Audit

This is a separate descriptive corpus study, not an RQ1b selector experiment.
It would define a source sampling frame, snapshot date, hash-deduplication and
source/domain strata; then measure each field's explicit, implicit or absent
source evidence, raw-Markdown position and field co-occurrence. A calibrated
independent audit sample is required before calling the output a prevalence
estimate. If only model-assisted coding is available, it must be labelled a
machine-assisted extractability audit rather than human ground truth.

The audit may support a bounded claim about the declared source frame, such as
the prevalence and placement of operational information. It cannot make the
near-neighbour benchmark representative of all public skills, or convert the
v2 effect estimate into a population-wide routing estimate.
