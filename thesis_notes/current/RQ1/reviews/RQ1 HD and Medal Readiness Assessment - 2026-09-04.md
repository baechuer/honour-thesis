## Current judgement

RQ1's two routing experiments are **First-Class/HD-capable**. Its strongest features are the paired controlled design, the complementary public-original removal experiment, the examples/tests negative control, cluster-aware uncertainty, and clear limits on what each result proves. The researcher has reviewed and approved all 946 controlled, public-gold, and public-removal units with the labels or edits visible. This confirms researcher agreement and case quality, but it is not an independent blinded review.

The completed 29,292-document model-assisted review is retained as descriptive supporting evidence: it shows that the proposed information types are common in the sampled public corpus, but it does not test routing. Its local package contains the summary and 31 batch totals rather than reusable per-document labels, so the thesis must not present it as a released annotation dataset. A separate length diagnostic also shows that some controlled gold fields, especially boundary/not-for and dependency/resource, are longer or more detailed than their alternatives. This is an ordinary limitation, not a failed experiment: the controlled study estimates the value of exposing field content as written, including its amount and specificity, rather than a pure length-normalised semantic effect.

## What is required for a defensible HD claim

1. Retain the 29,292-document review only as descriptive supporting evidence, backed by the retained summary and 31 batch totals; do not present it as routing evidence or a released per-document annotation dataset.
2. Preserve the completed researcher review: 350 controlled clusters, 194 scored public gold families, and 402 scored public transformations, all approved with receipts and labels or edits visible.
3. Keep the controlled claim within-field: every field helps relative to its own identical-context baseline, but the seven separate suites do not establish a formal field ranking.
4. Report that gold target-field wording is longer or richer in several controlled suites. State that the experiment measures the value of exposing the information as written, not a length-normalised semantic effect. A matched sensitivity set would be optional future strengthening, not a validity requirement.
5. State that public removal deletes all mapped field-bearing lines, may remove unequal amounts or overlapping functions, and measures routing sensitivity rather than edited-skill executability.
6. Say plainly that the benchmark was created with AI assistance under a researcher-defined rubric, mechanically checked, and reviewed and approved by the researcher; complete the required AI-use declaration before submission.
7. Keep the main RQ1 answer concise: which fields were positive in controlled isolation, which survived public removal under both retrievers, which were retriever-conditional, and which did not show a stable public effect.
8. Put full prompt-subset tables, historical experiments, and long protocol history in appendices or repository artifacts.
9. Finish the closest-work comparison and make novelty and interval wording evidence-bounded.

## What would strengthen a Medal case

The following are not required to make the present work valid, but they would materially strengthen a 90+ whole-thesis case:

1. A second, genuinely independent blinded reviewer for at least the public gold labels and a predeclared stratified sample of removal transformations, with agreement and adjudication reported.
2. One untouched external or held-out replication that was not used while designing the field taxonomy, clusters, prompts, or removal rules.
3. A release-grade reproducibility package that rebuilds the freezes, runs both retrievers, regenerates intervals, and verifies source hashes from one documented entry point.
4. A sharper theoretical synthesis connecting RQ1's field evidence to RQ2's representation and retrieval choices, including a prediction that RQ2 could falsify.
5. Exceptionally concise examiner-facing writing: one canonical result narrative, no stale status claims, and no requirement that the reader reconstruct the project's history.
