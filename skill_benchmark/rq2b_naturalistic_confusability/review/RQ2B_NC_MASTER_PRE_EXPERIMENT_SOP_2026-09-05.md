# RQ2b-NC master pre-experiment SOP

Date: 2026-09-05  
Status: **USER-APPROVED PRE-EXPERIMENT METHOD CONTRACT**  
Terminal state: **STOP AT `READY_FOR_FORMAL_EXPERIMENT`**

## 1. Master goal

Construct one new, immutable, reproducible and auditable `RQ2b-NC-final`
benchmark. Continue source-native expansion until at least 300 clusters have
passed every local gate and every batch started before the selected cut line is
closed. Then close and replay the source union, complete structural and
semantic-leakage QA, freeze the audit input, conduct the predeclared K=6 parent
delta and NC whole-library acceptable-set audit, close all remediations or
exclusions, materialise and validate I1/I2/I3-flat/I3C, and emit one final
manifest plus a formal-experiment readiness report.

This contract stops before any formal retrieval, embedding, reranking,
provider call, metric, or thesis-result update. Those actions require a later
explicit authorisation against the final manifest hash.

## 2. Authority and inherited contracts

This SOP consolidates and prospectively governs the path from the current
source-native expansion state to the formal-experiment boundary. It inherits
the non-conflicting requirements in:

- `RQ2B_NC_EXPANSION_TO_300_CHANGE_CONTROL_2026-09-04.md`;
- `ACCEPTABLE_SET_AUDIT_INPUT_FREEZE_AND_K6_SOP_2026-09-04.md`;
- `PARENT_V3_NC_FINAL_MERGE_AND_DELTA_AUDIT_CONTRACT_2026-08-31.md`;
- `FINAL_LIBRARY_FREEZE_ASSEMBLY_SPEC_2026-08-31.md`; and
- the applicable frozen RQ2b representation, selector and analysis protocol.

Where a historical count, cut line or preliminary execution plan differs from
this approved 300+ contract, preserve it as historical evidence and create a
new version. Never overwrite it.

Approval of this SOP authorises routine local pre-experiment construction,
review, reconciliation, validation and documentation within the stated
boundaries. It does not itself authorise paid calls, external source transfer,
hosted computation or the formal experiment.

## 3. Fixed boundaries

1. Historical V3 is a hash-bound parent and is never edited in place.
2. The 31k-scale public source frame is a discovery universe, not a bulk
   candidate admission.
3. RQ1 broad candidates, V3 candidates and NC sources retain separate origin,
   provenance and reporting strata.
4. The new candidate library contains one canonical candidate per exact source
   SHA-256. Historical aliases remain traceable but do not create duplicate
   candidates.
5. A cluster and a testcase are different units. A locally eligible source-native
   family has three source members and three active prompts, one targeting each
   member.
6. No partial-family counting is allowed.
7. A local gate establishes only three-source routing adequacy. It does not
   establish whole-library uniqueness, final gold or retrieval performance.
8. `K=6` is the maximum automatically proposed whole-library main pool. It may
   be found inadequate, but it must not be silently changed to K=8 or tuned
   after labels.
9. Candidate-proposal and tail scores are navigation or coverage evidence, not
   benchmark metrics.
10. No prompt, label, K value, candidate queue or inclusion rule may be changed
    using historical or new retrieval/reranking outcomes.
11. Every status count must be recomputed from canonical machine-readable
    ledgers. A prose summary or conversational count is not authoritative.

## 4. Phase 0 — reconstruct the current state

Before continuing expansion, the controller must derive and hash a current
state reconciliation containing:

- frozen checkpoint clusters;
- newly local-gate-eligible families;
- unadmitted, deferred, excluded and in-flight families;
- every previously inspected source hash, whether it passed or failed;
- source, family, active-prompt and batch counts; and
- current, historical, superseded, quarantined and helper-artifact status.

Any discrepancy between summaries and canonical ledgers must be resolved or
recorded as a blocker. The execution baseline is the recomputed ledger, not a
hard-coded starting count.

**Phase pass:** all reported counts replay from named records and hashes, and
no batch has an ambiguous state.

## 5. Phase 1 — source-native discovery to the 300+ cut line

Continue discovery from the frozen public frame under these rules:

1. Every new family is source-native and source-disjoint.
2. Any source hash inspected in an earlier batch is unavailable for new-family
   selection, regardless of its earlier disposition.
3. Lexical or embedding similarity may navigate the frame but cannot establish
   semantic confusability or admission.
4. Review uses the original full skill materials. A generated abstraction or
   source card cannot substitute for the source.
5. Families are selected without retrieval outcomes and cannot be chosen
   because they make a method look good or bad.
6. Gates are never relaxed to reach the numeric target.

### Expansion stopping rule

Stop new discovery only when both conditions hold:

1. at least 300 families have passed all local gates; and
2. every batch started before the selected 300+ cut line has received a final
   eligible, unadmitted, deferred or excluded disposition.

All eligible families from those already-started batches remain included even
if the closed total exceeds 300. Do not cherry-pick an exact set of 300.

## 6. Phase 2 — mandatory local-gate pipeline

Every candidate family must complete these gates in order.

### 6.1 Source-native family gate

- exactly three exact-source-unique original skills;
- a meaningful first-route decision boundary;
- no aliases, forks, transformed copies, containment-only relationships,
  generic/specialist hierarchy or component/lifecycle composition masquerading
  as independent alternatives.

### 6.2 Independent full-source review

Two reviewers independently read the complete original sources and judge the
family boundary. Exact agreement is retained. Every disagreement is sent to a
packet-only coordinator. Original returns remain immutable.

### 6.3 Provenance, licence and byte replay

For all three members record, where applicable:

- upstream repository or source URL;
- immutable commit or equivalent revision;
- source path and local replay path;
- source SHA-256;
- declared licence and licence-file SHA-256; and
- provenance disposition.

Missing bytes, unresolved identity, malformed source binding or unresolved
licence evidence fails closed or defers the family. A limitation stratum may be
retained only when explicitly permitted by the parent contract; it is not a
new provenance pass.

### 6.4 Source-supported prompt authoring

Author one natural operational prompt per member. Each prompt must be supported
by the source and distinguish the intended route using the task goal, input
object, required workflow, constraints or deliverable.

Do not expose a skill title, repository, provider, path, benchmark slug or an
avoidable source-unique phrase. A necessary framework or artifact cue must be
flagged as a reporting stratum rather than hidden.

### 6.5 Independent target-blind adequacy review

Two independent reviewers receive only the prompt and three source-visible
candidates under opaque tokens. They do not receive the intended target,
author mapping, proposal rank, previous review, benchmark role or retrieval
outcome. They judge each candidate using the frozen adequacy rubric. A
coordinator receives only sealed disagreement packets.

A prompt passes locally when its intended member is finally adjudicated
`MOST_SUITABLE`, its integrity/cue gate passes, and no evidence or packet defect
remains. A non-target may still be `FULLY_ACCEPTABLE` or
`PARTIALLY_ADEQUATE`; this must be recorded and does not by itself fail the
local prompt.

### 6.6 Family eligibility gate

A family is local-gate eligible only when all three source members pass and all
three active prompts pass. One or two passing prompts are retained as review
evidence but contribute zero eligible clusters.

### 6.7 Bounded remediation

Each failed prompt may receive at most one source-grounded, reason-specific
remediation under the unchanged rubric. Preserve the superseded version and
use fresh target-blind reviewers. If it still fails, retain the family as
unadmitted/deferred. A second remediation, source-universe change or rubric
change requires an explicit method amendment.

Original source bytes remain immutable. Any source-description overlay must be
source-grounded, separately versioned and freshly blind-reviewed; it never
replaces I2-original.

## 7. Phase 3 — close collection and run the fresh 300+ preflight

After the stopping rule, prohibit further discovery for this version and build
the prospective source-canonical union from:

- historical V3 source-canonical candidates;
- previously approved RQ1 broad-candidate strata;
- all locally eligible NC sources; and
- any explicitly bound legacy/checkpoint strata.

Exact-hash deduplicate the union. Do not bulk-add the discovery frame.

The fresh preflight must check:

- missing source and hash drift;
- exact duplicates, aliases, forks and transformed near-copies;
- source, provenance and licence replay;
- active/tombstoned lineage and quarantine status;
- cluster completeness;
- exact and semantic-near-copy prompts;
- parent/NC and other split leakage;
- target-title, repository, provider, path and literal-source cues;
- source-description drift and approved overlays;
- origin and difficulty/cue strata; and
- unchanged parent V3 identity and result bindings.

Unresolved local defects block affected records. A systemic defect blocks the
audit-input freeze.

## 8. Phase 4 — freeze the audit input and prospective analysis contract

Materialise a new versioned `RQ2b-NC-audit-input-300plus-YYYY-MM-DD` package.
Before any acceptable-set label is seen, seal and hash:

- the candidate, parent-prompt and NC-prompt manifests;
- source and provenance manifests;
- the exact outcome-blind candidate-proposal implementation, fields, depth,
  tie rule and environment;
- six opaque main-pool candidate tokens per applicable prompt;
- the deterministic map of up to two eligible tails per prompt group;
- reviewer packets and return schemas;
- reconciliation, adjudication and token-join maps; and
- the hypotheses, estimands, endpoints, strata, metrics, failure analysis,
  cost accounting and statistical plan for the later formal experiment.

Historical RQ2b methods may be inherited, but final counts, hashes and strata
must be rebound. Any change to a primary hypothesis, non-inferiority margin,
rerank budget, bootstrap plan or primary endpoint requires a prospective
amendment before selector outcomes are generated.

This package is an audit-input freeze, not a final benchmark or result.

## 9. Phase 5 — K=6 whole-library acceptable-set audit

### 9.1 Lane A: parent-381 delta audit

For each frozen parent prompt:

1. preserve its text, ID, historical gold and historical result binding;
2. review only source-unique candidates added after V3 through the sealed,
   outcome-blind alternative queue;
3. retain historical gold in a separate field;
4. add newly fully acceptable candidates to the new acceptable set;
5. record any more-suitable new candidate as a label-delta stratum without
   rewriting V3; and
6. exclude or block a prompt if its historical gold fails an approved validity
   audit. Never repair it after viewing a retrieval result.

### 9.2 Lane B: NC whole-library audit

Audit every locally eligible NC prompt against the complete final
source-canonical union. Every adjudicated `FULLY_ACCEPTABLE` candidate enters
the acceptable set. A cluster-local singleton becomes strict only when the
bounded whole-library process closes without an additional fully acceptable
route or unresolved coverage issue.

### 9.3 Reviewer and tail workflow

1. Two independent reviewers see the prompt and source-visible candidates
   under opaque tokens.
2. They do not see rank, intended target, author mapping, local label, another
   reviewer outcome or retrieval/reranking result.
3. Each candidate receives `FULLY_ACCEPTABLE`, `PARTIALLY_ADEQUATE`,
   `INADEQUATE` or `UNCLEAR`, plus a source anchor and rationale.
4. Exact agreement is retained; disagreement is adjudicated through a sealed
   packet-only coordinator.
5. Applicable tails use the same rubric. They test omissions rather than score
   a retrieval system.

Let `A(p)` be the finally adjudicated `FULLY_ACCEPTABLE` set:

| Condition | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| `|A(p)| = 1` and no unresolved quality/tail issue | `STRICT_FINAL_CANDIDATE` |
| `|A(p)| >= 2` and every member is recorded | `ACCEPTABLE_SET_FINAL_CANDIDATE` |
| `|A(p)| = 0`, `UNCLEAR`, failed provenance, leakage or missing evidence | `BLOCK_OR_REJECT` |
| fully acceptable positive tail outside closure | `METHOD_GATE_OPEN` |

A fully acceptable positive tail is a hard method gate. A partially adequate
or unclear tail is a recorded coverage/quality issue and cannot be silently
treated as a negative. Absence from a K=6 or tail packet means unlabelled, not
inadequate.

## 10. Phase 6 — post-audit remediation and exclusion

Permitted actions are limited to:

- repairing malformed packets, path references or clerical joins without
  changing semantic decisions;
- versioned repair of a prompt/source defect identified independently of
  retrieval outcomes, followed by fresh blinded review of the affected scope;
- preserving blocked versions; and
- excluding records that cannot be validly closed.

It is prohibited to revise gold after rankings, force a multi-acceptable prompt
into strict gold, selectively repair sampled rows, overwrite a failed version,
loop on prompt wording until it passes, or silently increase K.

Changing the construct, rubric, proposal depth or source universe requires a
new method amendment and whatever re-audit that amendment specifies.

## 11. Phase 7 — materialise and validate final representations

Preserve historical V3 representations unchanged. For each newly admitted
source, materialise:

- `I1`: source-native name plus original short description;
- `I2`: the complete original skill artifact;
- `I3C`: fielded operational facts extracted only from I2; and
- `I3-flat`: the same evidence-span multiset as I3C with field labels and
  boundaries removed.

Automatic integrity must establish:

- exactly one representation row per source identity;
- every selector-visible I3 evidence span is an exact I2 substring, subject
  only to the frozen whitespace policy;
- I3C and I3-flat evidence-span multisets match per source;
- no gold, role, family, stratum, target or QA metadata is selector-visible;
- no unsupported generated vocabulary, duplicate evidence, scaffold residue
  or identity mismatch; and
- final tokenisation and chunking conform to the prospective protocol.

Run at least 120 stratified blinded QA rows. Any critical identity, leakage,
unsupported-evidence or non-substring error rejects the extraction version.
More than 5% major errors overall or in any reviewed field stratum rejects the
version, triggers a blinded corpus-wide audit of the affected class, requires a
deterministic source-grounded repair and then a fresh sample. Do not repair only
the sampled rows.

External source transfer, paid embedding/provider calls and hosted computation
remain separate authorisation gates.

## 12. Phase 8 — final freeze QA

Only after all earlier gates close may the finaliser create the immutable
`RQ2b-NC-final-YYYY-MM-DD/` root containing:

1. parent V3 hashes and unchanged historical identity/result references;
2. one source-unique candidate manifest with origin/provenance strata;
3. parent prompts with historical gold and delta-audited final acceptable sets;
4. disjoint NC strict, acceptable-set and excluded prompt manifests;
5. all A/B, coordinator and tail ledgers;
6. provenance, licence and replay evidence;
7. I1/I2/I3C/I3-flat and their integrity/QA reports;
8. split, cue, duplication, semantic-near-copy and coverage reports;
9. model, instruction, script, environment and input hashes;
10. exact replay commands;
11. one root-manifest SHA-256; and
12. `FORMAL_EXPERIMENT_READINESS_REPORT.md`.

The final testcase count is:

```text
passed parent delta-audit prompts + passed NC whole-library-audit prompts
```

It is not an expansion-stage count or `clusters x 3`.

## 13. Phase 9 — terminal readiness state

The controller may emit `READY_FOR_FORMAL_EXPERIMENT` only when all of the
following are `PASS`:

- the 300+ stopping rule and all in-flight batch closure;
- complete source-union replay;
- parent delta audit;
- NC whole-library audit;
- K=6/tail closure with no open method gate;
- resolution, exclusion or explicit deferral of every blocked record;
- representation integrity and blinded QA;
- duplication, semantic-near-copy, cue and split-leakage QA;
- final manifest/hash replay; and
- prospective experimental and statistical protocol freeze.

At that boundary, report candidate, cluster and prompt counts; strict,
acceptable and excluded counts; strata; unresolved limitations; the final root
hash; estimated formal-run calls, time and cost; and proposed replay commands.

Then stop. Do not execute the formal experiment without a new explicit
authorisation tied to the final hash.

## 14. Reviewer-agent separation and auditability

1. A prompt author cannot blind-review that prompt.
2. Reviewer A and B for a packet must be independent.
3. A coordinator receives only the sealed disagreement packet.
4. Review agents return structured records and do not directly edit canonical
   ledgers.
5. The finaliser receives target/source joins only after all applicable blind
   returns are sealed.
6. Record each reviewer model, reasoning level, instruction hash, input hash,
   output hash and validation result.
7. Agent review is reported as AI-assisted structured review, not external
   human-expert annotation.

## 15. Autonomy and mandatory pause rules

The main agent may continue routine local discovery, review, provenance,
authoring, reconciliation, remediation, validation and documentation without
requesting approval batch by batch. It must maintain versioned ledgers and give
brief milestone updates.

It must pause for an explicit decision if it would:

- change K=6, the 300+ target, three-member family form or all-three gate;
- change the rubric, source universe or acceptable-set definition;
- proceed after a fully acceptable positive tail or unresolved systemic
  coverage issue;
- waive a systemic provenance, licence, lineage, leakage or integrity defect;
- send source or prompt text to a newly authorised external service;
- incur paid or hosted computation; or
- proceed beyond `READY_FOR_FORMAL_EXPERIMENT`.

If progress becomes low-yield, record the yield and continue to the approved
300+ stopping rule unless a genuine gate makes it methodologically impossible.
Difficulty does not by itself authorise weaker evidence standards.
