# USYD Computer Science Thesis Requirements Check

Checked on: 2026-05-27

This note records the public University of Sydney guidance checked while setting up the LaTeX thesis scaffold. It does not replace Canvas, the unit outline PDF, or supervisor instructions.

## Sources Checked

1. INFO4990: Computer Science Research Methods  
   https://www.sydney.edu.au/units/INFO4990

   Useful points:

   - The unit covers CS research methods.
   - Students learn to find and evaluate literature, present a research plan or results, write a literature review, learn research quality metrics and ethics.
   - Learning outcomes include planning research, critically evaluating work, retrieving academic literature, producing a literature survey, writing and presenting a proposal, understanding ethics/copyright, and communicating research ideas clearly.

2. Computer Science Honours unit of study table  
   https://www.sydney.edu.au/handbooks/science/table-a/subject-areas/computer-science/honours-unit-of-study-table.html

   Useful points:

   - Computer Science Honours is a 48 credit point program.
   - It includes a 24 credit point research project plus coursework, including CS research methods.
   - Research project units follow from INFO4990.

3. INFO4914: Computer Science Industry Placement Scholarship  
   https://www.sydney.edu.au/units/INFO4914

   Useful point:

   - Although this is a related industry-placement pathway rather than the normal thesis unit, its learning outcomes are useful as style guidance: it expects a literature survey, research proposal, original contribution, and thesis writing in a style suitable for academic literature.

## Scaffold Implications

The thesis folder should include:

- abstract;
- declaration/ethics/acknowledgements placeholders;
- introduction and research questions;
- literature review;
- conceptual framework;
- benchmark/design chapter;
- methodology;
- results;
- discussion/failure analysis;
- conclusion;
- appendices for rubric and prompt examples.

## Thesis-Specific Check

The current thesis framing fits the public guidance because it:

- has explicit research questions;
- is grounded in a literature review;
- defines a CS research problem;
- proposes a reproducible empirical methodology;
- includes quantitative evaluation and qualitative failure analysis;
- discusses ethics/copyright/reproducibility;
- aims for an original contribution through representation analysis, benchmark design, and empirical comparison.
